How to play

  • Move: drag on screen. Left/right turns, up walks.
  • 📸 Flash: banish ghosts in front of you.
  • 🏃 Run: hold to sprint (watch the ring).
  • Cross the road at the zebra. Cars stop for you there.
  • Collect 💰 coins, shop with ⭐, drive the red car.
  • Inside the house find 3 🔑 keys, read 📜 diary pages, then escape out the front door.
  • Hide in wardrobes when ghosts hunt you.
